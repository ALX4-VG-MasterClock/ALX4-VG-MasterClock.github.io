**Subject: Request for rectification of my personal information — Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1), section 28**

Dear Sir or Madam,

I ask you to rectify the following personal information you hold about me, in accordance with section 28 of the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector: [description of the information concerned and where it appears].

This information must be rectified because it is [inaccurate: it states “…” whereas it should state “…”] [incomplete: it omits “…”] [equivocal: “…”] [collected, communicated or kept in a manner not authorized by law: specify]. In support of the correction requested: [documents or explanations enclosed].

I ask you to send me, free of charge, a copy of the information modified or added or, as the case may be, an attestation of its deletion, as provided for in section 35. I understand that you must respond with diligence and not later than 30 days (section 32) and that, failing a response, you are deemed to have refused.

In the event of disagreement over the rectification requested, I remind you that in case of disagreement, the burden of proof rests with you (section 53), subject to the exceptions it provides, and I reserve the right to deposit my written comments in the file, as permitted by article 40 of the Civil Code of Québec.

[Signature or name]

---

**Legal basis of this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of rectification: Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1), s. 28 — rectification if the information is “inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal” or if its collection, communication or keeping is not authorized by law.
- Time limit: s. 32 — response with diligence and not later than 30 days; silence at the end of the time limit amounts to a deemed refusal.
- Receiving the corrected information: s. 35 — a granted rectification gives the right to a free copy of the information modified or added, or to an attestation of deletion.
- Reversed burden: s. 53 — in case of disagreement over rectification, the burden of proof rests with the holder, unless the information came from the person or was collected with the person's agreement.
- General regime: Civil Code of Québec, art. 40 — to cause information that is “inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal” to be rectified, to cause information that is “obsolete information or information not justified by the purpose of the file” to be deleted, or to deposit written comments in the file; the holder gives notice without delay to the persons who received the information in the preceding six months.
- Recourse: ss. 42 and 43 — application for an examination of a disagreement to the Commission d'accès à l'information, within 30 days of the refusal or of the expiry of the time limit to respond.
Sources: Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1) and Civil Code of Québec (CCQ-1991), texts read on LégisQuébec, verified on 3 November 2026 (register of legal bases, §2).

If it is refused, I ask you for a refusal with reasons in writing, with the text of the provision invoked and notice of the recourse (section 36), as well as the registration of my request for rectification with the information concerned (section 37), and its communication to the recipients of the information if I so require (section 68).

---

**Legal basis of this request — health variant** *(block printed in the document)*
- Right of rectification: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), s. 19 — right to cause information that is inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal, or collected or kept in contravention of the law, to be rectified.
- Form and time limit: s. 32 (written request to the person in charge, identity proved, assistance if the request is imprecise), s. 33 (written notice of receipt) and s. 34 — effect given “promptly and not later than 30 days”; failing that, deemed refusal.
- Refusal with reasons and trace of the request: s. 36 — every refusal is reasoned in writing, with the text of the provision invoked and notice of the recourse; s. 37 — if rectification is refused, the body must, on request, register the request for rectification with the information; s. 68 — that registration is communicated to the recipients if the person so requires.
- Receiving the corrected information: s. 67 — the body “must […] communicate to the applicant, free of charge, a copy of any amended or added information or, as applicable, an attestation of the deletion of information”, and, on request, to the persons to whom the information had been communicated.
- Burden on review: s. 141 — in rectification matters, it is the body that must prove that the information need not be rectified, unless it collected it directly from the person; s. 143 — the Commission may make the appropriate order.
- Review: s. 132 — application for review to the Commission d'accès à l'information within 30 days of the decision or of the expiry of the time limit to respond.
Sources: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), text read on LégisQuébec, verified on 3 November 2026 (register of legal bases, §2).

## Construction notes (do not appear in the document)

- **Choices that lead to this template**: purpose = correct one's data; regime = private enterprise, Québec (main document) or health, Québec (variant); the composer chooses the document according to the regime declared at the routing step — the two texts are never mixed in a single document.
- **Time limit started on the declared sending**: D+30 in both regimes, .ics calendar file generated (deadline, reminder, opening of the recourse — examination of a disagreement ss. 42/43 or review s. 132), entry in the chained proof journal with the fingerprint of the composed document.
- **Cross-cutting layer** (register of legal bases, §0): LCCJTI, ss. 5, 6, 11 and 17 — probative value of the composed document and of its transfer certificate.
- **What the template does not do**: it sends nothing (the user sends, rule 8); it promises no result; it does **not** include a “public body” variant — rectification under A-2.1 (s. 89) has not been re-read at the primary source and does not appear in the register tables; nor does it include an Alberta variant (Alberta's PIPA absent from the rectification tables). The federal rectification bases (PIPEDA, principles 4.9.5 and 4.6) and British Columbia bases (PIPA, s. 24) exist in the register but belong to a later batch.
