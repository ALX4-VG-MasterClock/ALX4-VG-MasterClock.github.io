P-39.1) if the platform is operated in Québec / Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (S.C. 2000, c. 5) in other cases]**

Dear Sir or Madam,

**A. Access.** I request access to the personal information you hold about me in connection with my use of your service, in particular: [my conversations and their history] [the files and documents I uploaded] [the media I had generated] [the logs and traces linked to my account: dates, devices, addresses]. For the Québec regime: section 27 of the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. For the federal regime: clause 4.9 of Schedule 1 to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, which provides that the organization informs the individual “of the use that is made of them and of the fact that they have been disclosed to third parties” and allows them to access their information.

**B. Use.** I request that you inform me: (1) of the use that is made and that has been made of my information, and of the third parties to which it has been disclosed, as precisely as possible (PIPEDA, clauses 4.9, 4.9.1 and 4.9.3; for Québec, of the purposes stated at collection, LRPSP, s. 8); (2) of the purposes for which my information was collected, and of any new purpose to which it may have been assigned since (PIPEDA, clauses 4.2.3 and 4.2.4; LRPSP, s. 8); (3) of your retention periods and your destruction rules (PIPEDA, clauses 4.5 and 4.5.2); (4) of the general description of the information you hold about users and of the use for which it is intended (PIPEDA, clause 4.8.2); (5) if your systems make use of identification, location or profiling technologies with respect to me, of the information provided for in section 8.1 of the LRPSP [Québec regime].

On model training, my question is as follows: is my information used, or has it been used, to train or improve your models? I link it to the identified purposes and to consent: information must not be used for purposes other than those for which it was collected, unless the person consents to it or the law requires it (PIPEDA, clause 4.5; specific purposes and consent: LRPSP, ss. 8 and 14). I therefore ask you to indicate to which identified and stated purpose this use relates, if applicable, and on what consent it rests.

[If applicable — Automated decision: I was the subject of a decision that seems to me to be based exclusively on automated processing of my information: [describe the decision and its date]. In accordance with section 12.1 of the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector [Québec regime], I ask you to inform me of this decision, of the personal information used to reach it, of the reasons and the principal factors that founded it, and I remind you of my right to have the information used corrected and to submit my observations to a person in a position to review the decision.]

I understand that you must respond within the time limit of my regime: [30 days (LRPSP, s. 32; PIPEDA, s. 8(3), extendable by no more than 30 days under conditions and with notice)], and that, failing a response within the time limit, you are deemed to have refused.

**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- (A) Access: LRPSP, s. 27 (enterprise operated in Québec); PIPEDA, Schedule 1, clause 4.9 (federal regime or outside Québec) — the same bases as the access request, applied to this holder. Time limits and deemed refusal: LRPSP, s. 32; PIPEDA, s. 8(3) to (5). Recourse of the applicable regime: LRPSP, ss. 42 and 43; PIPEDA, ss. 11(3) and 14.
- (B) Knowing the use — federal: PIPEDA, clause 4.9 — the organization must inform the individual “of the use that is made of them and of the fact that they have been disclosed to third parties”; clause 4.9.1 — it “shall provide an account of the use that has been made or is being made of this information and an account of the third parties to which it has been disclosed”; clause 4.9.3 — the account of third parties must be as specific as possible.
- (B) Identified purposes and new purposes — federal: clause 4.2.3 — the purposes must be specified “at or before the time of collection”; clause 4.2.4 — “When personal information that has been collected is to be used for a purpose not previously identified, the new purpose shall be identified prior to use” and consent obtained, except for a purpose required by law; clause 4.2.5 — the persons who collect must be able to “explain to individuals the purposes for which the information is being collected”.
- (B) Limits on use and retention — federal: clause 4.5 — “Personal information shall not be used or disclosed for purposes other than those for which it was collected, except with the consent of the individual or as required by law. Personal information shall be retained only as long as necessary for the fulfilment of those purposes”; clause 4.5.2 — the retention guidelines “should include minimum and maximum retention periods”.
- (B) Openness about practices — federal: clause 4.8.2 — the information made available must include “a description of the type of personal information held by the organization, including a general account of its use” and the nature of the information made available to related organizations.
- (B) Stated purposes — private sector, Québec: LRPSP, s. 8 — at the time of collection, the person must be informed of the purposes for which the information is collected; s. 8.1 — if the collection uses an identification, location or profiling technology, additional prior information and means offered to activate these functions.
- (B) Automated decision — private sector, Québec: LRPSP, s. 12.1 — where a decision is based exclusively on automated processing, the enterprise must inform the person of it; on request: the information used, the reasons and the principal factors that led to the decision, the right to have the personal information used corrected and the opportunity to submit observations to a person in a position to review the decision. *(Provision cited without a verbatim extract, in accordance with the register of legal bases.)*
- **Printed limit — “model training”**: no provision of the statutes cited (LRPSP, PIPEDA, PIPA, LRSS) names model training. The question asked in this letter rests indirectly on them — identified purposes and new purposes (PIPEDA, clause 4.2; LRPSP, s. 8), limits on use (PIPEDA, clause 4.5) and specific consent (LRPSP, s. 14) — and it is formulated as a question linked to those purposes, never as an autonomous right to know about “training”.
- First step (official documentation, not a right): before this letter, the pathway has the user carry out the platform's official export where one exists — Google Takeout (the “Gemini” box exports only the Gems; the conversations go through “My Activity → Gemini Apps”), OpenAI (export within up to 7 days, link valid 24 h), Apple (request via Data and Privacy, 14 days to download). None of these aids establishes that the export delivers the full text of the conversations; the export states what the platform declares it puts in it, this request exercises the right. Meta: export time limits and contents not verified on a primary page — nothing is asserted about them.
Sources: statutes cited, texts read on LégisQuébec and lois-laws.justice.gc.ca; official platform aids read in part 1; verified on November 3, 2026 (register of legal bases, §6).
