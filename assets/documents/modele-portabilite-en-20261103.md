# Template — Request for portability (autonomous multi-regime template, Québec) and federal bound

Status: template from the site's document library — workshop draft written on 3 November 2026, based on the register of legal bases of the same day. No publication, no sending.
Certainty rule: every provision cited below appears in the register of legal bases at the status “verified at the primary source” (text read on LégisQuébec); the interpretation of the Commission d'accès à l'information is cited as an interpretation, with the tension it carries. Structuring bound of the template: the PIPEDA in force contains **no** portability (absence established in the register) — there is therefore no federal portability document; this file tells the federal user why instead of producing a document without a basis for them. Bound: General information, not legal advice.
Planned composition on the site: local, in the visitor's browser; the fields in [brackets] are filled in with the visitor; at composition, the site calculates the fingerprint of the final document (design rule 3) and starts the time limit in the visitor's tracking file (rules 1, 2, 7).

**Legal basis of this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Private sector, Québec: Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1), s. 27, third paragraph — “Unless doing so raises serious practical difficulties, computerized personal information collected from the applicant, and not created or inferred using personal information concerning him, must, at his request, be communicated to him in a structured, commonly used technological format”; the communication may also be made, at the applicant's request, to a person or body authorized by law to collect such information.
- Public bodies, Québec: Act respecting Access (CQLR, c. A-2.1), s. 84, third paragraph — substantially identical provision.
- Health, Québec: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), s. 66, second paragraph — “unless doing so causes serious practical difficulties, such information, where collected from the person concerned and not created or inferred from information concerning him or her, must be communicated to the applicant in a structured, commonly used technological format”.
- Interpretation (cited as an interpretation, not as the law): Commission d'accès à l'information, page “Responsabilité des entreprises” [official English wording not retrieved — verify before use] — the Commission reads “recueillis auprès de la personne” [official English wording not retrieved — verify before use] broadly: directly or indirectly, including information generated by the person's activities (purchase history, movement history); information obtained from a third party is excluded; the time limit is the same as for access (30 days); no obligation of interoperability between systems is imposed. This reading is broader than the text of the law alone — the document says so.
- Time limits and recourses: those of the access request under the same regime (LRPSP, ss. 32 and 42/43; A-2.1, ss. 47, 98 and 135; LRSS, ss. 34 and 132).
Sources: Québec statutes, texts read on LégisQuébec; interpretation read on cai.gouv.qc.ca; verified on 3 November 2026 (register of legal bases, §3).

## What the site tells the federal user (text displayed instead of a document — no federal portability document is produced)

“Your holder is governed by the federal law (PIPEDA). That law, in its text in force on 3 November 2026, contains **no right of portability**: it does not allow you to demand your information in a structured, portable format. The data mobility right appears only in Bill C-36, introduced on 15 June 2026 and not adopted. The site will therefore not produce a federal portability document — it would be without a basis. What you can do: request access to your information and to be able to consult it (Schedule 1, principle 4.9 — see the federal access template); nothing prevents you from asking, in that framework, for a usable copy, but the organization has no federal obligation to provide it in a structured portable format.”

## Construction notes (do not appear in the document)

- **Choices that lead to this template**: purpose = leave with one's data; regime = private QC, public QC or health QC (the composer inserts the reference of the chosen regime in the subject line and in the time-limit paragraph); regime = federal → **no document**: the explanation text above is displayed and the journey offers the federal access template.
- **Time limit started on the declared sending**: that of the regime (D+30 private/health; D+20 + 10 public), .ics calendar file generated, entry in the chained proof journal with the fingerprint of the composed document.
- **Cross-cutting layer** (register of legal bases, §0): LCCJTI, ss. 5, 6, 11 and 17 — probative value of the composed document and of its transfer certificate; the layer takes on a particular meaning here: the fingerprint of the file received in portable format can be calculated on receipt and recorded in the user's file.
- **What the template does not do**: it sends nothing (the user sends, rule 8); it promises no result; it never presents the Commission's broad reading as the text of the law (the tension is printed); it produces no federal portability document (absence established at the primary source, register §3).
