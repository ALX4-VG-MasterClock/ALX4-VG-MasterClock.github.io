I request access to the personal information you hold concerning me, as well as the information on the use made of it and the names of the persons and organizations to which it has been disclosed, in accordance with section 23 of British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act.

To allow my identification, here is the information concerning me in your systems: [client or account number, associated email, telephone, other relevant identifiers]. I have proof of my identity available for you.

I understand that you must respond no later than 30 days following receipt of my request (section 29, subsection 1) and that any refusal on your part must indicate its reasons, the provision invoked and the route for review (section 30, subsection 1).

[Signature or name]

---

**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of access: Personal Information Protection Act (SBC 2003, c. 63), s. 23 — access to one's personal information, information on its uses and on the names of the recipients to which it has been disclosed.
- Time limit: s. 29(1) — response no later than 30 days after receipt of the request.
- Refusal: s. 30(1) — the refusal must indicate its reasons, the provision invoked and the route for review.
- Review: s. 47 — review by British Columbia's Information and Privacy Commissioner within 30 days of notification of the decision; s. 47(3) — this time limit does not apply to a failure to respond.
Sources: Personal Information Protection Act (SBC 2003, c. 63), text read on BC Laws (bclaws.gov.bc.ca), verified on November 3, 2026 (register of authorities, §1).
