**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of traceability: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), s. 18 — right to be informed of the name of any person or group having accessed the information, otherwise having used it or received communication of it, as well as of the date and time. This is the strongest traceability right in the corpus of Acts examined; the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector contains no general equivalent.
- Form: s. 32 — written request to the person in charge of the protection of information; the person proves his or her identity and capacity; the person in charge must lend assistance if the request is not sufficiently specific.
- Acknowledgement, time limit, deemed refusal: s. 33 — written notice of the date of receipt, of the time limits, of the effect of failure to comply with them and of the review proceedings; s. 34 — response “promptly and not later than 30 days”; failing that, the person in charge is deemed to have refused.
- Review: s. 132 — application for review to the Commission d'accès à l'information “within 30 days of the decision or of the expiry of the time limit for responding” (relief from failure to apply within that time possible for reasonable cause); s. 133 — written application briefly stating the reasons.
- Assistance and force of the decision: s. 136 — “The members of the Commission's personnel must lend assistance in drafting” an application for review to every interested person who requires it; s. 143 — the Commission may order that access be given; s. 147 — a decision ordering something to be done, filed in the office of the clerk, has the force and effect of a judgment of the Superior Court.
Sources: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), text read on LégisQuébec, verified on November 3, 2026 (register of authorities, §7).

## Construction notes (do not appear in the document)

- **Choices leading to this template**: regime = health, Québec; purpose = knowing who accessed one's information (traceability), distinct from the template for access to content (s. 17); period covered mandatory, with the option “all accesses recorded”.
- **Time limit started on declared sending**: D+30 (response; s. 33 acknowledgement expected in the meantime), calendar .ics file generated (deadline, follow-up, opening of review under s. 132), entry in the chained proof log with the fingerprint of the composed document.
- **Cross-cutting layer** (register of authorities, §0): LCCJTI, ss. 5, 6, 11 and 17 — probative value of the composed document and of its transfer certificate.
- **What the template does not do**: it sends nothing (the user sends, rule 8); it promises no result; it does not request the content of the information itself (that is the subject of the health access template); it has no equivalent in the other regimes in the primary sources read — no “register of accesses” template is produced for the private sector, the public sector, the federal regime or the other provinces.
