I understand that you must respond with diligence and not later than 30 days after receipt of my request (section 32), that access is free of charge subject to reasonable fees that must be announced to me in advance (section 33), and that any refusal must be reasoned and accompanied by the recourses available to me (section 34).

[Signature or name]

**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of access: LRPSP, s. 27. Where applicable, portability: s. 27, 3rd paragraph (computerized information collected from the applicant, not created or inferred — unless serious practical difficulties).
- Form: written request addressed to the person in charge of the protection of personal information, with proof of identity — s. 30.
- Time limit: response with diligence, not later than 30 days — s. 32. Silence at the expiry of the time limit is deemed a refusal.
- Fees: access free of charge; reasonable fees may be charged if they are announced in advance — s. 33.
- Refusal: it must be reasoned and accompanied by an indication of the recourses — s. 34.
- Recourse: in case of refusal or silence, application for the examination of a disagreement to the Commission d'accès à l'information — s. 42; time limit to submit it: 30 days — s. 43.
Sources: Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR, c. P-39.1), text read on LégisQuébec, verified on 3 November 2026.
