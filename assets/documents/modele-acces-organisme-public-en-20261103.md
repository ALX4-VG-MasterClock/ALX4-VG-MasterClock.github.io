I understand that you must respond not later than 20 days after receipt of my request and that any extension, of not more than 10 days, must be decided and notified to me in writing before the expiry of the initial time limit (sections 47 and 98), failing which the absence of a response is deemed a refusal.

**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of access: Access Act (CQLR, c. A-2.1), ss. 83 and 84 — to be informed of the existence of personal information and to receive communication of it; consultation on site or remotely and copy. Where applicable, portability: s. 84, 3rd paragraph (computerized information collected from the applicant, communicated in a structured, commonly used technological format).
- Time limit: ss. 47 and 98 — respond “not later than twenty days” after receipt; extension of not more than 10 days, decided and notified in writing before the expiry of the initial time limit; silence is deemed a refusal.
- Review: s. 135 — application for review to the Commission d'accès à l'information “within thirty days of the date of the decision or of the expiry of the time limit”.
- General support: Civil Code of Québec, arts. 38 and 39 — free examination of any file kept on oneself (reproduction at reasonable cost, intelligible transcript); the holder may not refuse “unless he has a serious and legitimate reason for doing so or unless the information may seriously injure a third person”.
- Privacy: Charter of human rights and freedoms (CQLR, c. C-12), s. 5 — “Every person has a right to respect for his private life.”
- Constitutional limit: Canadian Charter, s. 8 — “Everyone has the right to be secure against unreasonable search or seizure” — applies to the State only (s. 32).
Sources: Act respecting Access to documents held by public bodies and the Protection of personal information (CQLR, c. A-2.1), Civil Code of Québec (CCQ-1991) and Charters, texts read on LégisQuébec and laws-lois.justice.gc.ca, verified on 3 November 2026 (register of legal bases, §1).
