**Legal basis for this request** *(block printed in the document — it is what gives the text its weight)*
- Right of access: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), s. 17 — “Every person has the right to be informed of the existence of and to have access to any information concerning him or her that is held by a body.” Temporary refusal only if serious harm to health is likely (opinion of a professional, documented).
- Form: s. 32 — written request to the person in charge; the person in charge must provide assistance if the request is imprecise. Acknowledgement: s. 33 — written notice of the date of receipt, the time limits and the recourse.
- Time limit: s. 34 — give effect “promptly and not later than 30 days”; failing that, the person in charge is deemed to have refused.
- Free of charge: s. 66 — the body “must […] communicate to the applicant, free of charge, the information concerned and allow the applicant to examine it on the premises […] or by remote access and to obtain a copy of it”.
- Portability (if requested): s. 66, 2nd paragraph — “unless doing so causes serious practical difficulties, such information, where collected from the person concerned and not created or inferred from information concerning him or her, must be communicated to the applicant in a structured, commonly used technological format”.
- Recourses: s. 132 — application for review to the Commission d'accès à l'information within 30 days of the decision or the expiry of the response time limit; s. 136 — the personnel of the Commission must provide assistance in drafting the application for review; ss. 143 and 147 — the Commission may order that access be given, and its decision, filed at the office of the court, has the force of a judgment of the Superior Court.
Sources: Act respecting health and social services information (CQLR, c. R-22.1), text read on LégisQuébec, verified on 3 November 2026 (register of legal bases, §1 and §7).

- **Choices leading to this template**: regime = health, Québec (body covered by the LRSS); purpose = see one's data; portability variant checked or not.
- **Time limit started on declared sending**: D+30 (response; acknowledgement of receipt s. 33 expected in the meantime), calendar file .ics generated (deadline, reminder, opening of the review s. 132), entry in the chained proof log with the fingerprint of the composed document.
- **Cross-cutting layer** (register of legal bases, §0): LCCJTI, ss. 5, 6, 11 and 17 — probative value of the composed document and its transfer certificate.
- **What the template does not do**: it sends nothing (the user sends, rule 8); it promises no result; it applies only to bodies covered by the LRSS — private holders outside the LRSS, federal or from other provinces fall under the other templates in the library; the request for the access register (“who accessed my information”) is the subject of a separate template based on s. 18.
